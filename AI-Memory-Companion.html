Recall & Reflect: AI Memory Companion

View Live ↗ View Report ↗

Designed a high-fidelity mobile app prototype that uses AI to help users capture, reflect on, and strengthen everyday memories through personalized daily check-ins and memory exercises.

UX Designer & Prototype Developer

UX Design, Prototyping, AI, Mobile

Overview

Recall & Reflect is an AI-powered mobile memory companion designed to help users capture fleeting everyday moments, reflect on patterns over time, and strengthen recall through guided exercises. The app addresses the growing need for intentional memory practices in a fast-paced digital world, offering a calm, supportive experience personalized to each user.

Problem & Goals

Many people struggle to hold on to meaningful everyday moments — small wins, emotional insights, and daily experiences that fade quickly without intentional capture. The goal was to design an app that felt low-friction for daily journaling, surfaced useful reflections automatically, and offered gentle AI-driven nudges without feeling intrusive or clinical.

My Role

As the UX Designer and prototype developer, I led the end-to-end design process from problem framing through high-fidelity prototyping. I defined user personas, mapped core task flows, designed the screen architecture, and crafted the AI suggestion and weekly insight features that personalize the experience.

Discovery & Constraints

Discovery began with identifying the emotional and cognitive barriers to consistent journaling. Key constraints included keeping the daily check-in to under two minutes, ensuring the AI suggestions felt supportive rather than surveillance-like, and designing for one-handed mobile use. Accessibility considerations shaped typography, contrast, and tap-target sizing throughout.

Information Architecture

The app is organized into four primary areas: Home (daily check-in and recent moments), Your Space (personal settings and AI profile), Reflect (weekly insights and pattern summaries), and Exercises (guided memory strengthening activities). Navigation uses a persistent bottom tab bar to minimize cognitive load and allow quick task switching.

User Flows

Three primary flows were designed: logging a moment via the quick-capture prompt, reviewing the AI-generated weekly insight summary, and completing a memory exercise. Each flow was optimized for low friction, with smart defaults and a single primary action per screen. Edge cases such as skipping a day or dismissing an AI suggestion were mapped to ensure the experience remained encouraging rather than guilt-inducing.

Wireframes & Prototypes

High-fidelity prototypes were built in Figma covering all core screens: the daily check-in dashboard, moment capture flow, AI insight cards, Your Space settings, and the memory exercise library. Interactive prototypes were used to validate navigation patterns and micro-interaction timing with test participants.

Requirements & Acceptance Criteria

Design requirements specified a maximum two-tap path to log a moment, WCAG AA color contrast compliance, a consistent soft visual language to reinforce the calm brand tone, and AI suggestion copy that passed a plain language readability check. Privacy considerations required that all AI personalization be transparently communicated in the Your Space section.

Analytics & Iteration

Two rounds of usability testing were conducted with five participants each. Key iterations included simplifying the moment-capture prompt, reordering the weekly insight summary to lead with a positive observation, and adding a 'Moment Saved' confirmation screen to provide satisfying closure to the logging flow.

Outcomes & Next Steps

The prototype successfully demonstrated a low-friction, AI-assisted memory journaling experience. Participants responded positively to the weekly insight summaries and the memory exercise library. Next steps include engineering handoff with detailed component specifications, integration planning for the AI suggestion engine, and an expanded accessibility audit for screen reader compatibility.
