Service Intake Workflow Analysis

View Live ↗ View Document ↗

Evaluating intake pathways to uncover delays, handoff gaps, and reporting blind spots.

Role

Business Analyst

Timeline

7 weeks

Tools

Journey Mapping, SQL, Stakeholder Interviews, Process Mapping

Overview

The Challenge

A service operations team was experiencing delayed response times and inconsistent intake quality. The root causes were unclear — leadership suspected technology issues, but the real problems were process and communication gaps.

Project Value

Faster, more consistent service delivery

By mapping the full intake workflow from submission to resolution, analyzing data at each handoff point, and interviewing frontline staff, the analysis revealed specific bottlenecks and actionable improvements that reduced average resolution time.

Key Scenarios

📥

Intake submission

Analyzed how service requests are submitted across channels — online form, phone, email — and identified inconsistencies in data capture that caused downstream delays.

🔀

Handoff and routing

Mapped how requests move between teams, identifying where information was lost, duplicated, or delayed during routing decisions.

📊

Performance reporting

Identified gaps in the current reporting setup that masked bottlenecks from leadership — and recommended a SQL-based dashboard to surface key metrics.

Features & Process

How It Came Together

As-is process mapping

Facilitated workshops with eight stakeholders to map the complete intake process, producing a detailed swimlane diagram that exposed handoff gaps.

Data analysis with SQL

Queried the service management database to analyze intake volume, routing time, resolution time, and return rates — segmented by channel and request type.

Recommendations report

Delivered a prioritized recommendations report with process improvements, technology quick wins, and a proposed future-state workflow diagram.
