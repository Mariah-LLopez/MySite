AI Augmentation Documentation Project

View Live ↗ View Document ↗

Structured documentation and knowledge base for AI-assisted research workflows.

Role

Technical Writer & Knowledge Architect

Timeline

10 weeks

Tools

Markdown, SOPs, Confluence, Knowledge Base

Overview

The Challenge

A research team was integrating AI tools into their workflow but lacked standardized documentation. Each team member used different prompting strategies, output formats, and verification approaches — creating inconsistent results and onboarding friction.

Project Value

Consistent, reusable AI-augmented workflows

By documenting prompting frameworks, output templates, and verification checklists, the team gained a shared language and process for AI-assisted research — reducing ramp-up time for new members and improving output quality.

Key Scenarios

🤖

Prompt library access

Researchers can browse a categorized library of verified prompts and adapt templates for their specific research contexts.

📋

Standard output formats

Structured templates ensure AI-generated research outputs are consistently formatted, cited, and ready for peer review.

✅

Verification checklists

Step-by-step checklists guide researchers through validating AI outputs before incorporation into final deliverables.

Features & Process

How It Came Together

Workflow mapping and gap analysis

Shadowed researchers across three teams to map current AI usage patterns, identify inconsistencies, and define the scope of documentation needed.

SOP and prompt framework creation

Wrote 12 standard operating procedures covering prompt construction, output review, citation handling, and escalation paths for uncertain outputs.

Knowledge base architecture

Organized all documentation in Confluence with a topic-based IA, cross-linking between related SOPs, templates, and reference materials.
