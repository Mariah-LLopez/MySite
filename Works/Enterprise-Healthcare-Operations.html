Problem & Goals

Helping healthcare staff understand compliance before workflow errors occur

Healthcare staff entering enterprise environments often face a steep learning curve around systems compliance and the procedural logic of medical coding.

Errors in coding directly affect billing accuracy, audit outcomes, and patient records integrity. The goal was to create a clear, accessible eLearning experience that explains how coding workflows fit into the larger operational picture.

Discovery & Constraints

Simplifying complex healthcare compliance content without losing accuracy

Discovery included reviewing enterprise healthcare onboarding materials, HIPAA training requirements, and ICD-10 coding documentation standards.

A key constraint was managing content complexity. Medical coding involves nuanced procedural logic that must be simplified for a broad learner audience without sacrificing accuracy or compliance fidelity.

Content Complexity

The course needed to explain technical healthcare workflows in a clear, accessible format.

Compliance Accuracy

Instructional content needed to remain aligned with coding standards and regulatory expectations.

Time Constraint

The course needed to be completed in under an hour while covering systems orientation and core coding concepts.

Key Features

Enterprise Systems Orientation

The course explains the relationship between EHR platforms, billing systems, compliance frameworks, and healthcare documentation workflows.

Interactive Coding Scenarios

Scenario-based activities ask learners to select the correct code for a clinical description from multiple options, reinforcing coding decision logic.

Compliance Checklist Activity

A checklist activity helps learners identify the required documentation elements before submitting a coded claim.

Information Architecture & User Flows

Structuring the course around systems, coding, and compliance checks

The course is organized into three sections: healthcare enterprise systems overview, medical coding workflows, and compliance and quality checks.

Learners move through the sections sequentially, with coding scenarios embedded in Section 2 and a compliance checklist activity in Section 3.

Primary Learner Flow

Enterprise Systems Overview → Medical Coding Workflows → Interactive Coding Scenarios → Compliance Checklist → Section Assessments → Completion

Wireframes & Prototypes

Storyboarding compliance-critical learning content before development

A storyboard was developed prior to Articulate 360 development, mapping each slide’s instructional content, interaction type, and compliance-critical callouts.

The storyboard was reviewed by a healthcare operations subject matter expert to validate workflow accuracy and identify gaps in coding procedure coverage before full course development.

Requirements & Acceptance Criteria

Measuring learner understanding of systems, coding, and compliance requirements

Learning objectives specified measurable outcomes: learners should be able to identify the stages of the medical coding workflow, apply ICD-10 coding logic to a clinical scenario, and describe the compliance requirements governing coded documentation.

Assessment items were tied directly to these objectives, with an 80% passing threshold per section.

Analytics, Iteration, Outcomes & Next Steps

Refining coding scenarios and compliance terminology through review

Two iteration cycles were completed following SME and peer review. The first cycle refined the ICD-10 scenario activities to better reflect real-world coding complexity without exceeding the scope of entry-level competencies.

The second cycle added a glossary of key terms after learner feedback showed that abbreviations in the compliance section needed clearer definition.
