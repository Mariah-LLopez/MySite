Cross-Team Release Documentation

View Live ↗ View Document ↗

Standardizing release checklists and decision logs for product, support, and operations teams.

Role

Technical Writer & Business Analyst

Timeline

6 weeks

Tools

Confluence, SOP Templates, Decision Logs, RACI

Overview

The Challenge

A product's release process involved three teams — product, support, and operations — but each team used different checklists, communicated in different channels, and had no shared record of release decisions. This caused missed steps, miscommunications, and post-release incidents.

Project Value

Releases that go smoothly every time

By creating a shared release documentation framework — including a master checklist, decision log template, and RACI matrix — all three teams now work from the same playbook, reducing miscommunication and post-release incidents.

Key Scenarios

📋

Pre-release checklist

Product, support, and operations teams complete their respective sections of a shared checklist before every release — ensuring nothing is missed.

📝

Decision log

Every release decision is recorded in a structured log: what was decided, who decided it, and why — creating an auditable trail.

👥

Cross-team RACI

A RACI matrix clarifies who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed for each release activity — eliminating ambiguity.

Features & Process

How It Came Together

Current-state release audit

Shadowed three release cycles and interviewed 10 stakeholders across product, support, and operations to map the current process and identify failure points.

Template design and testing

Designed the master checklist, decision log, and RACI template with input from all three teams — piloting them on two releases before finalizing.

Rollout and training

Trained all three teams on the new templates through a 60-minute workshop and Confluence-based guide — achieving 100% adoption within two release cycles.
